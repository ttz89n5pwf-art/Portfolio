Projects

SHEET03.1 DISCIPLINEStructural — Prestressed Concrete CODEAS 3600

Loss of Prestress — Post-Tensioned Pedestrian Bridge

A 22 m simply supported post-tensioned pedestrian bridge in Hobart, analysed across three horizontal tendons. The assignment works through immediate and time-dependent prestress losses over a 10-year service life — elastic shortening, friction, anchorage draw-in, and long-term shrinkage, creep and relaxation — following the AS 3600 method.

  • AS 3600
  • Prestress losses
  • Section analysis
SHEET03.2 DISCIPLINEGeotechnical UNITBET401

Shallow Foundation Design & Assessment

Design and assessment of a shallow foundation as part of Geotechnical Engineering 2 — working through bearing capacity theory and foundation sizing against serviceability and ultimate limit states.

  • Bearing capacity
  • Foundation design
SHEET03.3 DISCIPLINEStructural — RC & Steel CODEAS 3600 / AS 4100

Structural Design Portfolio

A run of structural design work across reinforced concrete and steel: RC beam design for flexure and shear, RC two-way slabs, column interaction diagrams, steel beam design to AS 4100 including lateral-torsional buckling checks, a tapered welded I-beam capacity assignment, and multi-storey frame analysis using the stiffness matrix method.

  • RC design
  • Steel design
  • Frame analysis
